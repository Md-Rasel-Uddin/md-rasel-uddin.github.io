AIMS Lab · Ongoing

AIMScribe: AI-Powered Clinical Scribing and Prescription Intelligence for Bangladeshi Healthcare

Advanced Intelligent Multidisciplinary Systems (AIMS) Lab, IRIIC, United International University

AIMScribe is a healthcare AI research project that aims to build a clinical documentation system for real-world Bangladeshi healthcare. It combines doctor–patient speech processing, automatic speech recognition (ASR), speaker diarization, clinical NLP, large language models (LLMs) and prescription analysis.

The system turns consultations into structured clinical information, reduces the documentation burden on doctors, improves medication safety and supports more efficient clinical workflows. The project is also building a clinically validated Bengali doctor–patient consultation dataset containing consultation audio, transcriptions, speaker information and prescription-related data.

My role

  • Developing and evaluating ASR and speaker diarization pipelines for Bengali clinical conversations
  • Designing annotation and gold-standard protocols for doctor–patient consultation data
  • Building and validating a real-world Bengali clinical speech dataset
  • Developing clinical NLP and LLM-based systems for transcription, information extraction and prescription analysis
  • Working closely with physicians, intern doctors, biomedical engineers, researchers, hospitals and clinics
  • Studying real clinical workflows, consultation practices and documentation challenges
  • Supporting data quality control, annotation standards, research protocols and system validation
  • Contributing to clinically deployable AI tools for documentation and decision support

Work 03

BdSLW-11: Dataset of Bangladeshi sign language words for recognizing 11 daily useful BdSL words

Background
Many datasets existed for BdSL alphabets, numbers and characters, but almost none for sign words. I started collecting and building this dataset under the guidance of a teacher at my undergraduate institution.
Skills
Data collection, data visualization and analysis, Python programming.
Contribution
